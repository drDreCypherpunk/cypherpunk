§2 — why this exists

Most "privacy" in Web3 is a toggle, not a foundation

The name "cypherpunk" used to be a promise — that the tools you build put the person using them back in control. Somewhere along the way, most of Web3 stopped asking that question and started asking a different one: how fast can this grow. We're asking the first question again, with a small group of people who actually care about the answer.
