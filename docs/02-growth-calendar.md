# Growth calendar — 24 weeks

**Decided:** target is 4,000 leads (Discord joins + waitlist signups + social
follows, counted together) from ~400,000 organic website visits, over **24 weeks**
(originally planned as 12 weeks, then explicitly doubled to halve the weekly pace
— same totals, gentler ramp). Model: a flat ~1% visit-to-lead conversion, held
by compounding content volume, with one deliberate spike in the final week.

Full week-by-week table and charts live in the **Growth Calendar** artifact (see
`README.md` for link). This file is the model and structure behind it.

## Funnel
- 400,000 visits → 4,000 leads over 24 weeks, ~1% conversion held flat.
- Weekly visits ramp from 2,500 (week 1) to 52,500 (week 24, the spike week).
- Cumulative checkpoints: Phase 1 exit (week 8) = 35k visits / 350 leads.
  Phase 2 exit (week 16) = 137k visits / 1,370 leads. Phase 3 exit (week 24) =
  400k visits / 4,000 leads.

## Team split
- **Founder** — voice & credibility: build-log threads, weekly Substack essay,
  manifesto/culture takes, AMAs.
- **Content lead** — production: repurposes founder's long-form into
  Threads/Reddit/X, graphics, owns the calendar itself.
- **Community lead** — conversion: Discord modding, quest tracking, seed-DM
  outreach, UTM tracking, weekly funnel review.

## Content pillars
1. **Build Log** — real progress, screenshots. Credibility engine. (X, Discord)
2. **Privacy 101** — evergreen, educational, SEO-shaped. (Substack)
3. **Culture & Manifesto** — opinions, hot takes, shareable. (X, Threads)
4. **Community Voices** — member spotlights, UGC, social proof. (Discord, Threads)
5. **Drops & Quests** — scarcity mechanics, conversion engine. (Discord, Reddit)

## Weekly rhythm (repeats every week)
| Day | Ships | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Build-log thread (X); Discord weekly kickoff | Build Log |
| Tue | Reddit value-post (rotating sub); Threads repurpose | Build Log |
| Wed | Substack essay publishes; one-line X link | Privacy 101 |
| Thu | Manifesto/hot-take post; Discord community spotlight | Culture · Community |
| Fri | Discord quest board resets; teaser cross-platform | Drops & Quests |
| Sat | Light-touch UGC repost | Community Voices |
| Sun | Funnel numbers reviewed internally; optional AMA | — |

## Phase detail
- **Phase 1 (weeks 1–8) — Foundation & Seed**: run the seed-DM push, get the
  weekly rhythm on rails, first Substack post live weeks 5–6.
- **Phase 2 (weeks 9–16) — Momentum & Distribution**: 2–3 micro-influencer
  collabs, Reddit rotation across subs (each sub no more than once every 2–3
  weeks — this is what keeps mods happy), referral quest added ~week 15–16.
- **Phase 3 (weeks 17–24) — Spike & Scale**: forcing event announced week 17,
  countdown content weeks 19–22, small budget boost behind the best-performing
  post, event goes live weeks 23–24.

## Tracking discipline
- Every outbound link carries a UTM so visits/leads trace to the specific post.
- Every post carries exactly one CTA (Discord invite primary, waitlist secondary)
  — two CTAs in one post is how both underperform.
- Weekly review every Sunday against targets; >15% behind two weeks running is
  the trigger to pull a lever early rather than hoping the week-24 spike covers it.
- 72-hour return rate on new joins remains the quality check underneath the
  raw lead count.

**Known risk, stated plainly:** 400k organic visits in 24 weeks from a cold
start is the hardest number in this plan — it assumes the Substack SEO
compounding and influencer collabs actually land. Revisit weekly targets after
week 8's real numbers come in rather than treating them as fixed.
