# Growth calendar — 24 weeks, one full cohort cycle

**Rewritten 2026-09-16 for confirmed positioning** (see `05-open-decisions.md`
#7 and `01-launch-plan.md`): this replaces the earlier 24-week "grow a broad
crypto/Web3 Discord community" calendar. That version optimized for
**community joins** from **broad organic visits**; this one optimizes for
**qualified founder applications** to a **12-week Bitcoin accelerator
cohort**, from a much smaller, higher-intent audience of Bitcoin builders.

**Why the shape of the calendar changed, not just the numbers:** a Discord
community can grow on a flat weekly ramp indefinitely. An accelerator can't —
it runs on a fixed cycle: an application window with a hard deadline, a
selection period, the 12-week program itself, and a Demo Day. The 24 weeks
here map to **one full cycle**, not an arbitrary flat build.

## Funnel
- **Target: ~150 completed applications → 10–15 accepted founders** (a
  realistic accelerator intake size) from an estimated ~15,000–20,000 funnel
  visits/impressions across the 24-week cycle. This is a ~1% visit-to-
  application rate — lower absolute volume than a community funnel, but
  each conversion is far higher-intent (a founder completing an application
  is a materially bigger commitment than a Discord join).
- These are placeholder planning numbers, not measured or committed —
  revisit after the first real application window closes (end of week 8)
  with actual data, same discipline as the old calendar's week-8 checkpoint.
- Cumulative checkpoints:
  - **Phase 1 exit (week 8, application deadline)**: application window
    closes. Target ~150 completed applications.
  - **Phase 2 midpoint (week 14–16)**: cohort is running; next cycle's
    pipeline (waitlist/newsletter signups) building quietly.
  - **Phase 3 exit (week 24, Demo Day)**: cohort founders present; next
    cohort's application window opens off the back of Demo Day momentum.

## Team split
- **Founder(s)** — voice & credibility: build-in-public threads, direct
  warm-network outreach to prospective applicants, mentor/investor
  relationships (Fulgur Ventures / Initial Capital network), Demo Day
  hosting.
- **Program/content lead** — production: application-page copy, cohort
  progress updates during the program, Demo Day materials, owns this
  calendar.
- **Community lead** — pipeline: Hub upkeep for the wider Bitcoin builder
  audience, next-cohort waitlist, event outreach, application-funnel
  tracking (source of each application: warm intro vs. outbound vs. inbound
  from the site).

## Content pillars (rebuilt for founder audience, not general crypto/Web3)
1. **Founder Build Log** — real progress from inside the cohort once it
   starts (weeks 9–20). Credibility engine. (X, Hub)
2. **Applicant Criteria & Program Details** — what Cypherpunk looks for,
   what founders get, deadline reminders. Conversion engine during the
   application window (weeks 1–8). (Site, X, direct outreach)
3. **Bitcoin Builder Culture** — opinions/analysis relevant to Bitcoin
   founders specifically (not general crypto takes). Credibility + reach.
   (X)
4. **Portfolio/Alumni Voices** — spotlights on companies that came through
   the program (CoS tab). Social proof for the next cohort. (Site, X, Hub)
5. **Demo Day & Events** — event-specific promotion, investor/press outreach
   ahead of Demo Day (weeks 21–24). (X, direct outreach, Hub)

## Weekly rhythm — differs by phase (not one flat rhythm across all 24 weeks)

**Weeks 1–8 (application window) — conversion-focused:**
| Day | Ships | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Direct outreach batch to warm-network founders | Applicant Criteria |
| Tue | X build-in-public post (why this cohort, why now) | Bitcoin Builder Culture |
| Wed | Application-criteria/FAQ post; deadline reminder | Applicant Criteria |
| Thu | Portfolio/alumni spotlight (proof this program works) | Portfolio/Alumni |
| Fri | Event/hackathon presence or follow-up from one | Applicant Criteria |
| Sun | Application funnel reviewed: sourced-by-channel breakdown | — |

**Weeks 9–20 (cohort running) — credibility-building, low-pressure pipeline:**
| Day | Ships | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Cohort progress update (with founders' consent) | Founder Build Log |
| Wed | Mentor session takeaway or milestone hit | Founder Build Log |
| Fri | Hub touchpoint for wider builder audience (light) | Portfolio/Alumni |
| Sun | Pipeline check: next-cohort waitlist growth, no hard push | — |

**Weeks 21–24 (Demo Day) — event-focused:**
| Day | Ships | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Demo Day countdown + founder teaser | Demo Day |
| Wed | Investor/press-specific outreach batch | Demo Day |
| Fri | Public RSVP push (X, Hub, direct) | Demo Day |
| Post-event | Recap + "applications for next cohort open" CTA | Applicant Criteria |

## Phase detail
- **Phase 1 (weeks 1–8) — Application Window**: direct founder outreach is
  the primary channel, not broad organic content. Hard deadline at week 8.
- **Phase 2 (weeks 9–20) — Cohort In Session**: marketing volume drops
  deliberately; the program itself (and founders shipping) is the content.
  Next-cohort pipeline builds quietly in the background, ramping in the
  final 2–3 weeks of this phase.
- **Phase 3 (weeks 21–24) — Demo Day**: the single forcing event of the
  cycle. Investor and press outreach front-loaded (weeks 21–22), public
  push weeks 23–24, next cycle's application window opens immediately after.

## Tracking discipline
- Every application is tagged by source (warm intro / direct outreach /
  event / inbound-organic) — this matters more here than raw UTM volume,
  since the pool is small enough that channel quality is the whole game.
- One CTA per touchpoint: "Apply as Founder" during the window, "Join the
  Hub / waitlist" for anyone not ready, never both at once.
- Week-8 review against the ~150-application target is the trigger to
  adjust outreach intensity for the remaining weeks of Phase 2/3 rather
  than waiting for Demo Day to reveal the shortfall.
- Quality check underneath the raw application count: **interview-to-accept
  rate and accepted-founder shipping velocity during the program** — an
  accelerator that fills seats with weak applicants because it chased volume
  has failed regardless of how the application number looks.

**Known risk, stated plainly:** the ~150-application and ~15–20k visit
figures are unverified planning assumptions, not measured data — this is a
much smaller and harder-to-reach audience than "crypto/Web3 broadly," and a
cold-start accelerator has no track record yet to point applicants to.
Revisit every number here after week 8's real application count comes in.
